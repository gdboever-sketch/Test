id: summarize-and-confirm
name: Summarize and confirm
category: information
summary: Restate what has been agreed and what is open, so progress is locked in and misunderstandings surface early.
use_when:
  - Several terms have been discussed and the ledger is getting complex
  - The counterpart's statements conflict with earlier ones
  - Before moving to a new issue, or before the call ends
signals:
  - Many offers in the ledger with unclear status
  - "Wait, I thought we said..."
avoid_when:
  - Nothing concrete has been discussed yet
---
## Moves
- Suggest a short recap using the offer ledger: agreed, open, and the next issue to tackle.
- Ask for an explicit "yes, that's right" on each agreed point.
- Use the recap to set the agenda: "the remaining gap is support terms."

## Say it like this
- "Let me check where we are: two years, net 45, and we still need to close on price. Is that right?"

## Watch out
- Summarise accurately. A slanted recap destroys trust when it's noticed.
