---
id: interests-behind-positions
name: Find the interests behind positions
category: information
summary: Ask open questions to learn why the counterpart wants what they ask for. Interests open up options that positions block.
use_when:
  - The counterpart repeats a demand without explaining it
  - The negotiation is stuck on a single number
  - Early in the meeting, before offers harden
signals:
  - '"We need..." or "We require..." with no reason given'
  - The same position stated twice
avoid_when:
  - The user is in the final minutes and needs to close
---
## Moves
- Suggest "what" and "how" questions, which invite explanation, rather than "why", which can sound accusatory.
- Note every interest revealed under counterpart_signals: quarter-end targets, risk, internal politics, cash flow.
- Use what is learned to propose options that meet their interest at less cost to the user.

## Say it like this
- "What's most important to you about the three-year term?"
- "How does this deal fit into what you're trying to achieve this quarter?"
- "What would make this a win on your side?"

## Watch out
- Let them answer fully. Suggest silence after each question.
