---
id: strategic-silence
name: Strategic silence
category: information
summary: After an offer, a question, or a "no", stop talking. Silence invites the other side to fill it, often with concessions or information.
use_when:
  - Right after the user makes an offer or asks a key question
  - The counterpart has just made an offer the user doesn't like
  - The user tends to keep justifying or softening after stating a position
signals:
  - The user keeps talking after naming a number
  - The user adds "but we could be flexible" unprompted
avoid_when:
  - The silence would read as hostile in a fragile relationship moment
---
## Moves
- Suggest that the user states the point once, then waits.
- When the user negotiates against themselves (softening without any pushback), raise a warning.
- After a disappointing offer, a pause followed by "Hmm" often prompts an improvement.

## Say it like this
- (Say nothing for a few seconds.)
- "Hmm." Then wait.

## Watch out
- On a phone or video call silence feels longer. A few seconds is enough.
