name: Label emotions and mirror words
category: information
summary: Name the feeling you hear and repeat key words as a question to defuse tension and draw out more information.
use_when:
  - The counterpart sounds frustrated, anxious, or defensive
  - The counterpart drops a loaded word ("fair", "final", "difficult") without explaining
signals:
  - Raised tone or emphatic language in the transcript
  - Short, clipped answers
  - The word "fair" used against the user
avoid_when:
  - Labelling would sound patronising in a very formal or technical exchange
---
## Moves
- Label: start with "It sounds like..." or "It seems like..." and name the emotion or concern. Then pause.
- Mirror: repeat the last one to three important words with a rising tone, then wait.
- Use these before making any argument; people listen better once they feel heard.

## Say it like this
- "It sounds like there's real pressure to close this quarter."
- "Your final offer?"
- "It seems like the support costs have been a sore point."

## Watch out
- Don't overuse it. One label or mirror per exchange is enough.
