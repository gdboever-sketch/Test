---
id: selective-disclosure
name: Selective, reciprocal disclosure
category: preparation
summary: Share interests and reasons freely, guard numbers and constraints, and trade information one piece for another.
use_when:
  - The counterpart asks about budget, deadlines, alternatives, or internal constraints
  - The user is about to mention something on the do-not-disclose list
  - Information has been flowing only one way
signals:
  - "What's your budget?"
  - "When do you need this by?"
  - "Who else are you talking to?"
avoid_when:
  - The information is harmless and sharing it builds trust (for example, why an issue matters)
---
## Moves
- Raise a critical alert if the user starts to state a do-not-disclose item or their walk-away number.
- Answer questions about limits with interests instead: why something matters, not how far the user can go.
- Make disclosure reciprocal: "Happy to go into that. What's driving the number on your side?"
- If something sensitive has already slipped out, suggest reframing it as a range or tying it to conditions, not denying it.

## Say it like this
- "Budget depends on what's included. Let's agree on the scope first."
- "We're looking at a few options. What matters to us is the total value over two years."

## Watch out
- Never suggest lying about alternatives or budgets. Declining to share is honest; inventing is not.
