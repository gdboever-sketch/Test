name: Anchor first, with reasons
category: opening
summary: When you know the market, open with an ambitious but defensible number and explain it.
use_when:
  - A number has not been put on the table yet and the user knows the market range
  - The counterpart invites the user to name a figure
avoid_when:
  - The user has little information about value, so an anchor could leave money on the table
  - The counterpart has already anchored (use counter-anchor instead)
signals:
  - "What did you have in mind?"
  - "Where would you like to start?"
---
## Moves
- Suggest a precise opening number at or slightly beyond the user's target, never past what they can justify.
- Lead with the rationale (benchmarks, value delivered, competing costs), then the number.
- Precise numbers ($382k rather than $380k) signal that analysis is behind them.
- After anchoring, suggest the user stops talking and lets the counterpart respond.

## Say it like this
- "Given the usage we've seen and current market rates, we're looking at $382k a year."

## Watch out
- An anchor so extreme it can't be explained damages credibility and can end the conversation.
