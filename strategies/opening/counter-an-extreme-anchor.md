name: Counter an extreme anchor
category: opening
summary: Don't negotiate from their number. Name it as out of range, ask for its basis, and re-anchor on your own terms.
use_when:
  - The counterpart opens far from the user's target
  - The user starts to make counteroffers relative to the counterpart's number
signals:
  - A price increase on a renewal
  - An opening number well outside the brief's range
  - "Our standard price is..."
avoid_when:
  - The counterpart's opening is already near the user's target
---
## Moves
- Suggest not countering immediately. A quick counter legitimises their anchor as the midpoint.
- Ask how the number was calculated, then discuss the basis instead of the figure.
- Re-anchor with the user's own justified number and reasons.
- Flag it if the user's counter splits the difference with an extreme anchor.

## Say it like this
- "That's well outside what we can consider. How did you arrive at it?"
- "Let's set that aside. Here's how we're thinking about value."

## Watch out
- Stay courteous. The goal is to reset the frame, not to insult the counterpart.
