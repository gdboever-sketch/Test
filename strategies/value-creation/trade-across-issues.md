---

name: Trade across issues
category: value-creation
summary: Give on issues that matter more to them than to you, and get on issues that matter more to you. Negotiate packages, not single terms.
use_when:
  - The discussion is stuck on one issue, usually price
  - The counterpart has revealed a priority that is cheap for the user to meet
  - The brief lists tradeables
signals:
  - Back-and-forth on one number with small moves
  - The counterpart mentions timing, case studies, payment terms, or volume as important
avoid_when:
  - The issue in question is a red line
---
## Moves
- Link issues: suggest that nothing is agreed until everything is agreed.
- Offer an item from the brief's tradeables in exchange for movement on a high-priority issue.
- Use the issue priorities in the brief: trade low-priority items for high-priority ones, never the reverse.

## Say it like this
- "If we can sign before your quarter closes, we'd need the price at $395k."
- "Let's look at the whole package rather than settle price on its own."

## Watch out
- Don't give a tradeable away for free just because it's cheap for the user. It is still currency.
