---
id: contingent-terms
name: Contingent terms
category: value-creation
summary: 'When the sides disagree about the future, turn the disagreement into a bet: terms that change depending on what happens.'
use_when:
  - The counterpart justifies a price with promised results the user doubts
  - The sides disagree on forecasts such as usage, uptime, or delivery dates
signals:
  - "You'll definitely see that value"
  - "Our uptime is always above 99.9%"
  - Disagreement about how much the user will use
avoid_when:
  - The contingency can't be measured or verified simply
---
## Moves
- Suggest tying payment or price to a measurable outcome: service credits for missed SLAs, price tiers by usage, or bonuses for early delivery.
- If they are confident, they should accept the bet; if they refuse, that reveals their real expectations.

## Say it like this
- "If you're confident about the 4-hour response, you won't mind service credits when it's missed."
- "Let's set the price by usage tier, so we both pay for what's real."

## Watch out
- Keep the measure simple and agreed in writing. Disputes over vague contingencies cost more than they save.
