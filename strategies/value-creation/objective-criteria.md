---
id: objective-criteria
name: Use objective criteria
category: value-creation
summary: Move the argument from willpower to standards (market rates, benchmarks, precedent, cost data) that both sides can accept.
use_when:
  - The counterpart justifies a position with "that's our price" or "that's policy"
  - The discussion becomes a contest of wills
  - The user needs to justify their own number
signals:
  - "That's our standard rate"
  - '"Take it or leave it" in an early phase'
  - Repeated insistence without reasons
avoid_when:
  - The user has no credible benchmark to cite
---
## Moves
- Ask what standard their number is based on.
- Suggest benchmarks from the brief's context, such as current price, market quotes, or inflation.
- Propose agreeing on the method first ("let's base it on price per active user"), then the number.

## Say it like this
- "What's that based on? Let's agree on a fair way to measure it."
- "Comparable contracts are coming in around 15% lower. How does yours compare?"

## Watch out
- Don't cite figures the user can't back up. And respect do-not-disclose items: "market rates" is safer than an exact competitor quote.
