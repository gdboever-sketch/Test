---
id: multiple-equivalent-offers
name: Multiple equivalent offers
category: value-creation
summary: Put two or three packages on the table that are equally good for you and let them choose. Their choice reveals their priorities.
use_when:
  - Several issues are in play and the counterpart's priorities are unclear
  - The counterpart keeps rejecting single offers
  - The user needs to make a counteroffer without looking like they are just conceding
signals:
  - '"That doesn''t work for us" with no counterproposal'
  - Multiple open items in the offer ledger
avoid_when:
  - Only one issue is being negotiated
---
## Moves
- Suggest two or three packages that are each acceptable to the user, varying the issues (for example price against term length against payment terms).
- Ask which one is closest and what would need to change.
- Record which package they lean towards under counterpart_signals.

## Say it like this
- "We could do $390k for two years, or $375k for three years with a 3% cap. Which works better for you?"

## Watch out
- Every option must be one the user would genuinely accept. Check each against the walk-away points.
