id: test-the-close
name: Test the close
category: closing
summary: When the gaps are small, ask a hypothetical closing question to find out exactly what stands between you and agreement.
use_when:
  - Most issues are agreed and one or two gaps remain
  - The counterpart's concessions are shrinking
  - Time in the meeting is running out
signals:
  - The ledger has mostly accepted items
  - "We're getting close"
avoid_when:
  - Major issues are still open; a premature close invites a hard no
---
## Moves
- Suggest a hypothetical close: "If we could solve X, would you be ready to sign?"
- If they say yes, solve X within the walk-away points. If they say no, ask what else remains.
- For a final small gap, consider bracketing it with a trade rather than splitting the difference unconditionally.

## Say it like this
- "Suppose we can meet you on payment terms. Is there anything else stopping us from agreeing today?"

## Watch out
- Check the final package against the brief before encouraging a yes. Raise an alert if any term is past a walk-away point.
