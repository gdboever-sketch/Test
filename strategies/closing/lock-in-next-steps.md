---
id: lock-in-next-steps
name: Lock in next steps
category: closing
summary: End every meeting with a spoken recap, named owners, dates, and who sends the written summary.
use_when:
  - The meeting is winding down
  - Agreement in principle has been reached
  - The counterpart says they need to check internally
signals:
  - "Let me take this back to my team"
  - "Let's touch base next week"
  - Goodbyes beginning
avoid_when:
  - (Always applies near the end of a meeting.)
---
## Moves
- Suggest a recap of agreed terms and open items, using the offer ledger.
- Ask who needs to approve on their side, and by when.
- Suggest that the user offers to send the written summary. Whoever writes it controls the wording.

## Say it like this
- "To recap: two years at $395k, net 45, with the SLA still open. I'll send that in writing today. Who needs to sign off on your side, and when can we expect it?"

## Watch out
- "Let's touch base" is not a next step. Push gently for a date.
