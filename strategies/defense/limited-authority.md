---
id: limited-authority
name: Handle limited authority and good cop, bad cop
category: defense
summary: When someone absent or tough "won't allow it", find out who decides and make sure your concessions are conditional on final approval.
use_when:
  - The counterpart blames an absent manager, committee, or policy
  - Two people on their side play friendly and tough roles
signals:
  - "I'd love to, but my director will never approve it"
  - '"Let me check with my boss" after each concession'
  - One person is warm while another is harsh
avoid_when:
  - The counterpart has clearly stated their approval process at the start
---
## Moves
- Suggest asking early who has final authority and what they care about.
- Make the user's concessions conditional on final approval too: "subject to sign-off on both sides".
- When good cop, bad cop appears, address both as one team and stay focused on interests.

## Say it like this
- "Understood. Who else needs to approve this, and what will they be looking at?"
- "Then let's treat everything as provisional until both sides have approval."

## Watch out
- Watch for being asked for a concession that is then escalated for another one. Count the rounds.
