---
id: take-it-or-leave-it
name: Respond to "take it or leave it"
category: defense
summary: Treat a final offer as a position, not a fact. Ignore or reframe it, and keep the conversation on interests and options.
use_when:
  - The counterpart says an offer is final or threatens to walk away
signals:
  - "This is our final offer"
  - "Take it or leave it"
  - "We'll have to walk away"
avoid_when:
  - The user's BATNA is weak and the offer is inside the walk-away points; then a pause to evaluate may be best
---
## Moves
- Raise a warning naming the tactic.
- Suggest not replying to the ultimatum directly. Move to a question or a new package so they can step back without losing face.
- Compare the offer against the BATNA in one line, so the user knows what walking away would cost.
- If the offer is past the walk-away point, the user can calmly accept leaving; that is often when movement happens.

## Say it like this
- "I hear that this is where you are. Let's look at whether a different structure gets us both there."
- "If that's truly final, we'll need to consider our alternatives. Before we do, can we look at term length?"

## Watch out
- Never suggest a bluff about walking away the user isn't prepared to carry out.
