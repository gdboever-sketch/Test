---
id: deadline-pressure
name: Respond to deadline pressure
category: defense
summary: Test artificial deadlines instead of accepting them, and never let a deadline force a term past your walk-away point.
use_when:
  - The counterpart says the offer expires soon or must be signed today
signals:
  - "This price is only valid until Friday"
  - "We need to sign this week"
  - "My manager will only approve it today"
avoid_when:
  - The deadline is real and also serves the user's interests (it can be a tradeable)
---
## Moves
- Raise a warning naming the tactic.
- Suggest asking what drives the deadline. Real deadlines have reasons, such as quarter-end.
- If the deadline serves them, treat meeting it as a concession the user can trade.
- Remind the user that their BATNA doesn't expire on Friday.

## Say it like this
- "What happens on Friday that changes the price?"
- "We could move quickly, but meeting your quarter-end is worth something to you. What can you do on price if we do?"

## Watch out
- Don't call the deadline a bluff outright. Ask questions and let the reasons, or their absence, speak.
