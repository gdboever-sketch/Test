name: De-escalate heat
category: defense
summary: When emotions rise, slow down, acknowledge, and separate the people from the problem before going back to the substance.
use_when:
  - The counterpart is angry, accusatory, or sarcastic
  - The user's replies are getting sharper
signals:
  - Interruptions, raised voices, or personal remarks in the transcript
  - Accusations such as "you're not being serious"
avoid_when:
  - The tension is mild and productive
---
## Moves
- Suggest a label for the emotion ("It sounds like this has been frustrating") and a pause.
- Suggest the user lowers their pace and volume. Calm is contagious.
- Propose a short break or moving to an easier issue to rebuild momentum.
- Raise a warning if the user starts conceding to end the discomfort.

## Say it like this
- "It sounds like we've hit a nerve. Let's take a step back. What's most important to fix here?"
- "Shall we take five minutes and come back to this?"

## Watch out
- Acknowledging an emotion is not agreeing with a demand. Keep both separate.
