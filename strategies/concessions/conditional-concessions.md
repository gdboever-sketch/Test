name: Conditional concessions
category: concessions
summary: Never give something without getting something back. Phrase every concession as "if you..., then I...".
use_when:
  - The user is about to concede on any term
  - The counterpart asks for something without offering anything
signals:
  - "Can you do a bit better on...?"
  - The user says "OK, we can do that" with nothing in return
avoid_when:
  - A small goodwill gesture is planned deliberately to unblock a stalled relationship
---
## Moves
- Before any concession, suggest putting the condition first: "If you..., then we could...".
- Raise a warning when the user concedes unconditionally; suggest attaching a condition retroactively ("...as long as we also agree on X").
- Use the brief's tradeables as the "then" side, and high-priority issues as the "if" side.

## Say it like this
- "If you can move to net 60, we can look at a three-year term."
- "We could consider that, if the SLA moves to four hours."

## Watch out
- Leading with the condition matters. "We'll do X if you do Y" invites them to pocket X.
