---
id: guard-against-nibbles
name: Guard against nibbles
category: concessions
summary: When small extra requests appear after the main terms are settled, price each one or bundle them into the package.
use_when:
  - The main terms are agreed and the counterpart asks for "one more small thing"
  - Requests trickle in one at a time
signals:
  - "Just one more thing..."
  - "Can you also throw in..."
  - New items appearing after a summary
avoid_when:
  - The request is genuinely trivial and closing quickly matters more
---
## Moves
- Suggest putting a value on the request and asking for something in return.
- Suggest asking for the complete list before agreeing anything else.
- Remind the user that the earlier agreement was a package; reopening one part reopens the rest.

## Say it like this
- "Before we go there, is there anything else on your list? I'd like to see it all together."
- "We can add that, and we'd need the payment terms at net 60 to make it work."

## Watch out
- Keep it friendly. A nibble is often habit, not hostility.
